# Privacy Policy for "Robot Factory"

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

**Developer:** 4isper

## 1. Overview
"Robot Factory" (the "App") is an idle/clicker game about building robots. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the App and how it is protected. We follow a minimal-data approach: the App does not require an account and does not upload your gameplay to any server.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device
The App processes game data — the factory state, resource amounts (metal, electronics, robots), upgrade levels, prestige currencies, settings, and progress — solely to provide the gameplay experience.
- All game data is stored **strictly locally** on your device. On Android it is stored in encrypted form (Android Encrypted Preferences); in the web version it is stored in the browser's local storage. We do not transmit, upload, or store this data on any external server under our control.
- The App does not request or store personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

## 3. Third-Party Services
### Advertising (Yandex Mobile Ads)
To support development and keep the App free, it integrates the **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK** (interstitial, rewarded, and native formats).
- **Consent.** On first launch the App asks for your consent to show personalized ads. If you consent, the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID / GAID) may be used to tailor ads. If you decline, ads are shown in a non-personalized, contextual form and the identifier is not used for profiling. You can change this choice at any time in the App settings.
- **Data processed by Yandex.** Yandex may process a minimal amount of anonymized technical data required to deliver and measure ads: device information (model, OS version, language), coarse location derived from the IP address (to serve ads in the correct language/region), and ad interaction events. This data may be processed by Yandex and its partners outside your country of residence. Yandex processes it independently under its own Privacy Policy, which covers Yandex Mobile Ads: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/.

## 4. Data Protection and Your Rights
- **Local data.** Because your game progress is stored only on your device, you retain full control over it. You can delete all App data at any time by clearing the App's data/cache in your device settings or by uninstalling the App entirely.
- **Advertising identifier.** You can reset or opt out of the advertising identifier at any time in your device system settings (Settings → Privacy → Advertising), and disable personalized ads in the App settings.
- **Withdrawing consent.** You may withdraw or change your advertising-consent choice at any time in the App settings.
- **EEA users.** If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data and to object to or restrict its processing, as well as to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, these rights are exercised through the controls above (and by uninstalling the App).

## 5. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for a general audience (age rating 13+) and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. By default all advertising is served in a non-personalized form until the user explicitly grants consent. If you believe information about a child has been collected, please contact us and we will remove it.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We advise you to review this page periodically.
